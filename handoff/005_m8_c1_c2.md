## 1. Summary

- C1 is built and passes every criterion that M7 wrote for it: ten champions on the full map, the clock, intents that end with an outcome, the role duty state, an observation boundary, snapshots, replays, and invariants (F1).
- C2 is built: one lane class for three lanes, three tower tiers, the five wave modes, minion damage on champions, and separate ledger entries for minion gold, support-item gold, and passive gold. Every C2 criterion of M7 passes (F17).
- C2 is not complete. Three required wave behaviours are unresolved: a stack of two waves, a hold that a champion can sustain, and a bounce in mid (F6, F8, F10).
- What does work: a crash, a bounce in side lanes, and in one setting of eight a delayed crash with one reinforcement (F7, F10).
- The lane is an exact port of M7's lane, checked on all 998 of M7's metrics. I then added the documented rules that M7 left out, and the review led to two fixes where M7's lane broke a documented rule (F3, F13 to F15).
- The traced mechanism: a wave with an advantage wins in 10 to 18 s, walks to the tower, and dies about 5 s before its reinforcement arrives (F5). When a diagnostic weakens minion blows, the missing behaviours appear. That fight length is the cause is my inference (F11).
- Two real-game measurements bear on it. A match would confirm two timings. It would not show that stacks and freezes are rare in the real game (D1).
- I lowered no bar of M7 and added no rule to make a behaviour appear. The unmet behaviours are strict expected failures in the test suite.
- The full suite gives 329 passed and 4 expected failures: M7's known prototype defect and three wave tests. A mutation runner breaks the code in 57 ways, and the tests catch all 57 (F20).
- My judgment: the foundation is ready for the next campaign, C3 to C5. The wave model is not validated (§7.3).

## 4. Departures from the specification

1. **An eleventh module.** M7 lists ten modules. I added `riftgame/economy.py`, because gold and XP rules fit neither the lane, which holds no champion state, nor the tick loop.
2. **A second ruleset file.** The added constants are in `patch_26_22_m8.toml`. Editing `patch_26_22.toml` would have marked all five M7 results files stale without changing a number.
3. **Death, respawn, and regeneration in C1 and C2.** M7 places death in C5. Minion damage can kill, so the kernel has a minimal death and the reference death timer. Kill rewards stay in C5. Health regeneration is applied each tick, which M7's tick order did not list.
4. **Collision is not a rule of the lane.** The owner asked me to investigate it where relevant. I judged a one-dimensional queue unfaithful and tested a cap instead (F13).
5. **Two changes to M7's lane behaviour.** The M8 lane keeps the target priority on every retarget and orders simultaneous champion attacks by a keyed draw (F15). Both move the lane toward documented behaviour. Neither was written to produce a wave behaviour, and neither produced one.
6. **Return actions.** A laner in debt may walk to nodes of its own lane, its base, or its fountain, as the owner asked. I excluded the walk to the enemy base, which the delegate's first version allowed.
7. **Delegated code that I then changed.** After the delegate finished I edited `world.py` (lane inputs, events, gold and XP, regeneration, the ruleset id), `champions.py` (wave-aware `lane_post`, the return set), `harness.py` (a farming policy), and four of its test files (the pin, the return set, the lane post, the stub lane's signature).
8. **An incident during delegation.** The delegate's first mutation runner edited the working tree in place, and one break stayed in `riftgame/world.py` for about ten minutes. It then moved the runner to a private copy and checked the file hashes. My wave runs at that time did not import `world.py`, and I reran everything later. The runner in the repository works only on a temporary copy.
9. **The roadmap.** The owner asked for the M7 closeout and settled decisions only. I edited six places: the status line, a closeout paragraph under M7, a progress note and seven binding decisions under M8, risk 11, the next actions, and a v0.3 entry. After the review the decisions follow the owner's words. They are still my selection from the owner's prompt.
