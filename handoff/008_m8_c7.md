### 3.2 Implementation correctness, strategic validation, real-game validation

**Correct inside the simulator.** A test shows each rule, and a mutation shows that the test can fail. That covers F1 to F6, the referee's rules in F8 and F9, and the earlier checkpoints.

**Validated strategically only as a sanity check.** The scripted policies beat random actions by a wide margin and use every subsystem, so the mechanics respond to sensible play. That says nothing about good strategy. I wrote the policies and their thresholds while I read the referee's output. Of the wave capabilities, crashing and bouncing appear. Accumulation and sustained holds appear in a small minority of settings, mostly against an absent opponent, and their verdicts flip with the tick.

**Not validated against the real game.** Nothing is. Every champion number, the mana numbers, the ward spots, and the policy thresholds are placeholders. The three measurements of the M8 document's section 5.8 would test the lane.

## 4. Departures from the specification

1. **Pursuit replaces a step of M7 section 5.2.** M7 moves champions in a fixed order within step 3. All champions now move at once from one snapshot, and a fighter's lead is a new rule (F1). The alternatives of 007/D6 were (a), close to this, and (b), an order by who chases whom. I chose (a) because (b) keeps a leak for rings.
2. **The policies are per side and per seat.** `harness.tick` takes one policy or one per seat. A policy built for blue reads places in blue's frame and needs the side to turn them back into map places.
3. **The referee changed the kernel once and itself four times.** In the kernel, minions could step up to 65 units past a standing tower while they chased a target behind it, which the `tower_blocks` rule now stops (off in `M7_LANE`). In the referee, a respawn's level is the level before the tick, because a champion that kills its killer on the same tick takes the XP after its timer is set. The tower check became one per standing tower. After the independent review it reads where the lane held each champion when the towers fired, checks every shot on a champion, and skips no tick. It also faults a wave, a camp, or a respawn that never comes.
4. **The baseline games run 20 minutes and read gold and XP at 15.** In 15-minute games 12 of 13 outer towers fell in the last 75 s, which left the tower clause almost no window.
5. **Two strict expected failures were dropped** (F17). M7 and the owner ask for the capabilities. They do not ask for 12 minions or for this one mid crash to bounce.
