## 1. Summary

- A faster fighter now recovers contact after a gap or a slow on lanes, jungle paths, and base edges, from its team's memory alone. Not chasing under a lethal tower is a separate rule of the scripted policy (F1, F2; Verified).
- Three tick-start delays in the lane caused the wave cases that collapsed at 0.5 s. With them fixed, the first-wave fight is the same at four ticks (F3, F4; Verified by tests, traces Reported).
- I made 0.25 s with a decision every 2 s the research clock, for fidelity. It is nearer the 0.125 s reference than 0.5 s but not converged, and the throughput gate fails there at 267 against 300 (F6; Verified).
- C8 is built: the information state, the hypothesis generator, saved scenarios, comparisons, two styles, and four scenarios. Changing hidden truth changes only the hindsight line (F7 to F10; Verified).
- C8 makes no recommendation. Three scenarios rank as stable trade-offs. In the fourth the ranking is uncertain, and its one stable dominance rests on 2 s differences with no spread (F11; Verified).
- The independent review on two models found four real defects after my first finish: near-dead respawned enemies, too small an error on the weighted line, a double walk, and a generator that gave up. All are fixed and rerun (§3.4; Verified).
- C2 stays open. Reinforcements accumulate in 0 of 48 settings on blue and 1 on red, and four campaign-4 pins are now strict expected failures. No threshold changed (F12; Verified).
- Several tests were restated or re-seeded, and three are weaker than before. §3.5 lists every change (Verified).
- The suite gives 982 passed and 9 expected failures, the mutation runner catches 413 of 413 mutations, and every results file is current (F14; Verified).
- My judgment: the implementation is complete apart from C2 and the throughput gate, and ready for the audit. The demo needs no code change (§7.3; Inference).

### 3.3 Remaining defects, and modelling assumptions

**Known defects and limits of the implementation.** Each is a place where the code does less than M7 asks or than the game does. Each is Verified in code or a run unless its row says otherwise.

| ID | Concern | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | C2 is open (F12). | No wave strategy can be drawn from M8. |
| C2 | The generator cannot explain a level that needs kill or assist XP, a Scuttler, or a second pass of camps. It refuses such a state rather than invent data. Over 416 sampled team states it gives up in 6, down from 16, and all 6 also fail on the code before the fix (Reported, the delegate's sweep). One test state, `REFUSED` in `tests/test_hypotheses.py`, is refused for a related cause: walks from the fountain ignore Homeguard, a clear ignores the ready attack, and a camp is taken at today's average level. | A scenario in such a state is refused at load. |

| C5 | A position override takes no new look. The team's memory keeps what it knew, while the moved champion's sight shows camps differently in 2 of 5 probed moves (Reported, the reviewer's probe). No C8 recipe uses one. | A recipe that moves a champion can disagree with itself about camps. |

| C10 | Saved scenarios are pickles. | Load only files you made. |
| C11 | A fighter's one-step lead can run straight through a fork inside a lane for one tick. | At most one step of overshoot. |
| C12 | The verdict rule has no minimum effect size. A difference with no spread across rollouts is clear however small, and 4,155 of 6,424 paired cells have no spread. | A stable dominance can rest on trivial deterministic differences, as in `freeze_push_shove`. |
| C13 | The side of an untouched lane's late drift depends on the tick, in the same way in nearly every seed (F5). It is not traced. | Side bias in long untouched runs is a property of map and tick together. |
| C14 | The throughput gate fails at the research clock: 267 against 300 (F6). | Training at 0.25 s needs a faster kernel or a decision on the gate. |

**Modelling assumptions.** These are choices, not defects. M8 §12 lists every one.
- The generator's activity weights and numbers.
- A visible enemy minion or tower is seen in full, attack state included.
- A realized world has no enemy wards.
- The two styles, standard and aggressive, are not labelled rank-typical.
- The 5 percent family level.
- The 300-unit arrival radius.
- The three lane rules, read as the continuous-time behaviour the lane approximates.
